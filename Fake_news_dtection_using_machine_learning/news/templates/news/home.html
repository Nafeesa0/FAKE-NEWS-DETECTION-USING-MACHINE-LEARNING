The Process

From Article to
Verdict in 4 Steps

Our rigorous AI pipeline ensures every result is consistent, reliable, and backed by science.

1
Submit Your Article

Paste any news content into our verification tool, or browse directly from our curated feed of pre-verified articles from top outlets.

2
NLP Preprocessing

NLTK cleans and tokenizes the text: stopword removal, punctuation stripping, and WordNet lemmatization prepare it for the neural network.

3
AI Model Analysis

Our Bidirectional LSTM processes word sequences in both directions, capturing context and linguistic patterns that characterize fake versus real news.

4
Instant Verdict

Receive a clear authentic/fake classification with a confidence score, probability breakdown, and actionable recommendations — in under 2 seconds.
